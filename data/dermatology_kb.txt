Eczema (atopic dermatitis): Itchy, dry, red or brownish patches, often on the inner elbows, behind the knees, hands and face. Skin can crack or ooze when scratched. It often starts in childhood and comes and goes. Triggers include dry skin, harsh soaps, stress and allergens. Daily moisturizing and avoiding triggers help. See a doctor if the skin shows pus or yellow crusts, or does not improve.

Psoriasis: Thick red patches covered with silvery-white scales, commonly on the elbows, knees, scalp and lower back. It may itch or burn, and nails can become pitted. It is a long-term condition that flares up and settles. It is not contagious.

Acne: Pimples, blackheads and whiteheads on the face, chest and back, caused by pores blocked with oil and dead skin. Painful deep lumps (cysts) or scarring need a dermatologist.

Ringworm (tinea): An itchy, red, ring-shaped scaly patch with a clearer centre. It is a fungal infection that spreads through skin contact, shared towels and pets. Antifungal creams are commonly used. See a doctor if it spreads, or if it is on the scalp or nails.

Contact dermatitis: A red, itchy rash where the skin touched an irritant or allergen such as soap, nickel jewellery, plants or cosmetics. Blisters can appear. It improves once the trigger is removed.

Hives (urticaria): Raised, itchy welts that appear and fade within hours and can move around the body. Causes include allergies, infections, heat and stress. EMERGENCY: hives together with swelling of the lips, face or tongue, trouble breathing or dizziness need urgent medical care.

Scabies: Intense itching that is worse at night, with tiny bumps or thin burrow lines between the fingers, on the wrists and around the waist. It is caused by mites and spreads through close contact. It needs prescription treatment, and close contacts may need treatment too.

Cellulitis (skin infection): Red, warm, swollen and painful skin that spreads, sometimes with fever. It needs prompt medical care.

Melanoma warning signs (ABCDE): A mole that is Asymmetrical, has an irregular Border, has uneven Colour, is larger than about 6 mm in Diameter, or is Evolving (changing). See a doctor soon.
